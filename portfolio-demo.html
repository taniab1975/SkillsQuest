Proposed Years 10–12 pathway and assessment threshold

Students could start in Year 10 and complete the program at any point through Year 12. The proposal allows submission for assessment after 55 logged learning hours. Reaching 55 hours does not automatically mean successful completion or an award.

The aim is up to four endorsed-program unit equivalents that may contribute to the WACE C-grade requirement, subject to SCSA approval, the equivalence granted and applicable limits. 55 hours does not mean four units. SCSA determines equivalence using the program’s average learning time and scope; the proposed pathway to any further units still needs design.

SCSA WACE Manual 2026, §§5.8.6–5.8.7. SkillsQuest is not yet endorsed.
