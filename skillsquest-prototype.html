Coordinating a handover

24 June 2026
Read STAR reflection
Situation
Our event team changed shifts during lunch.
Task
Help the next group take over.
Action
I asked each teammate what still needed doing, agreed who would own each job and wrote a shared handover.
Result
The next group picked up all three jobs without asking us to return.

Initiative

Testing a new pickup layout

5 Aug 2026
Read STAR reflection
Situation
A later stall had the same congestion.
Task
Improve the layout before the busiest break.
Action
I sketched two layouts, asked the supervisor to approve a trial and counted how often the queue crossed the pickup area.
Result
Crossings fell from six to two in the same five-minute window. I shared the layout for the next team.

Communication

Adapting a visitor briefing

16 Sept 2026
Read STAR reflection
Situation
At a school open day, visitors were unfamiliar with campus names.
Task
Help families reach the workshop.
Action
I swapped building names for visible landmarks, asked a family to tell me their next turn and adjusted my wording when they hesitated.
Result
The next three families followed the route without needing a second explanation.
